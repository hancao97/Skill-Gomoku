Five recorded Yunzi go-stone placements on wooden go boards.
First three: tabletop board with wooden legs.
Last two: shin-kaya 19x19 thin board.
Recorded with a Marantz Professional Umpire microphone.

This project uses the source page's high-quality public MP3 preview,
cut into five 44.1 kHz PCM WAV impacts. Edits: onset trimming,
DC removal, level normalization and tail fades. No synthesized
tone, pitch shift or reverb is added to the stone placements.
